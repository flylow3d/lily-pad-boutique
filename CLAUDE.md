## Design direction — "enchanted twilight pond" (style #22, new personality)

Geared entirely to their brand artwork (refs/brand-art-crop.png): a crystal
chandelier glowing over a giant lily pad bearing a cream tufted armchair,
dark pond at dusk, fireflies, pink water lilies, golden script lettering.

- Palette: deep midnight indigo/pond teal base, warm chandelier gold,
  water-lily blush pink, lily-pad green. Dark, luminous, storybook-magical
  (distinct from Leo Barber's dark vintage — this is soft/enchanted).
- Type: Great Vibes (script, brand + accents) + Marcellus (headings) +
  Mulish (body). Google Fonts via <link> in HTML, never @import.
- Signature motifs: firefly glow dots (CSS), gold hairline flourishes,
  lily-pad divider, chandelier sparkle. CSS-built brand mark.
- Imagery: Gemini, dark enchanted-pond editorial style, --ref the brand
  crop for mood. Credits RESET for August (tested 2026-08-06, works).
