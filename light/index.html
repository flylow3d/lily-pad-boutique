Welcome in

Settle onto the Lily Pad

Some shops you run into. This one, you sink into. The Lily Pad Boutique is a new shop opening its doors on West Main Street in Gas City — a place for the things that make a house feel warm: decor that turns with the seasons, everyday pieces you'll keep forever, touches for the porch and garden, and baby clothes soft enough to make you stop mid-aisle.

The shop takes over a cozy Main Street storefront in the heart of town — the same downtown that strings itself with holiday lights each winter in "The Original Boom Town."

Doors haven't opened just yet. The best way to be there when they do is to follow the shop on Facebook — that's where the opening announcement will land first.

Three Little Worlds

Seasonal & Everyday Home

Decor that turns with the calendar — and the everyday pieces that stay put. Candles, florals, throws, and the finishing touches that make a room feel done.

Outdoor & Garden

Lanterns, planters, and porch-and-patio pieces — because the magic shouldn't stop at the back door.

Baby & Littles

Soft little sweaters, rompers, and gifts for the newest arrivals — the corner of the shop nobody walks past without touching something.
